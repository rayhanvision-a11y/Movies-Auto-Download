Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about MOVIESFLIX Engine

1. What is MOVIESFLIX Auto-Downloader?

It is a zero-ad, high-speed media link extraction engine. It bypasses aggressive redirects, popups, and shortlinks to grab direct .MKV / .MP4 video download links across all active sites.

2. How do I auto-download files directly to Windows IDM?

Click the Send to IDM button on any file. The engine will communicate directly with IDMan.exe on your Windows system to add it to your download queue instantly!

3. What video resolutions are extracted?

All video qualities including 4K (2160p), 1080p Full HD, 720p HD, and 480p SD are supported and labeled clearly. HEVC files are filtered out for maximum compatibility.

4. How do I use BDIX FTP servers?

Click FTP Servers in the top menu to auto-scan active BDIX servers in Bangladesh and add them to your site list.
